## 1. Project Title
**ClauseGuard AI — Adversarial Contract & Dark-Pattern Shield**

## 2. One-Line Tagline
A 3-stage adversarial AI contract auditor that X-rays Indian rental, job, and freelance agreements for hidden traps and drafts instant counter-clauses.

## 3. Problem Statement
Students, renters, freelancers, and early-career developers regularly sign agreements containing predatory clauses—₹2.5L startup bonds, unenforceable 2-year non-competes, 100% deposit forfeiture, and Net-90 IP grabs—because legal review is expensive and generic AI summaries lack Indian legal context and actionable redlines.

## 4. Solution & Prompt Engineering Strategy
ClauseGuard AI runs a **3-Stage Adversarial Prompt Pipeline** powered by **Gemini 4 Hydrogen (`gemini-4-hydrogen`)** via `@google/genai`:
- **Stage 1 (Clause Extractor & Taxonomist):** Isolates obligations and anchors exact verbatim quotes for inline UI highlighting.
- **Stage 2 (Adversarial Red-Team Auditor):** Cross-examines clauses against Indian legal principles (Section 27 & 74 of the Indian Contract Act 1872, Model Tenancy Act, Consumer Protection Act 2019) and scores 4 risk vectors.
- **Stage 3 (Fair-Draft Negotiator):** Outputs drop-in counter-clauses plus a polite, ready-to-send Email and WhatsApp negotiation script.
